Global Kuranishi charts seminar

This is a physical seminar, taking place Tuesday at 11:00 in Ross 70 (April-June 2026). The organizsers are Keren and Arye.
The goal is to study Complex cobordism, Hamiltonian loops and global Kuranishi charts (by Abouzaid, McLean, Smith)
The format is a reading seminar; each week a speaker will prepare and present few pages from the paper.

21 April: Keren - Intro and few definitions (4.1-4.?)

28 April: May - Topology of (micro)bundles and Kuranishi str. (4.? - 4.6)

5 May: Asaf -From Rational Homology to Generalized Orientations (5.1 - 5.4)

12 May: Arye - Morava K-theory and Ambidexterity (5.5 - 5.6)

2 Jun: Or - Bonus: Smoothing theory

23 Jun: Keren - Constructing the Virtual Fundamental Class (5.7 - 5.8)

?? : Oded - Constructing the Global Chart for Unmarked Curves (6.1 - 6.2)

?? : Or - The Gromov Trick and Sheared Almost Complex Structures (6.3, 6.4, 6.6)

?? : Benzion - Transversality via Peak Sections (6.5 and 6.7)

?? : Elad - Gluing, Marked Points, and Invariants (6.8 - 6.12)
