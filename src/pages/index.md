I am a PhD student at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, supervised by [Tomer Schlank](https://sites.google.com/view/tomer-schlank) and [Yoel Groman](https://math.huji.ac.il/~ygroman/).

My mathematical fields of interest include **Homotopy theory**, **Differential topology**, **Category theory**, and **Floer theory**.

Currently, my research focuses on generalizing ideas and results from Floer theory by utilizing the new language of Floer homotopy theory. Feel free to reach out to me at: [arye.deutsch@mail.huji.ac.il](mailto:arye.deutsch@mail.huji.ac.il).
