# Floer homotopy seminar

This page contains materials from the **Floer homotopy seminar** (September–December 2024). Organized by **Arye Deutsch**, **Sebastian Haney**, and **Trygve Poppe Oldervoll**, it was aimed at PhD students and postdocs in Homotopy Theory and Symplectic/Floer Theory.

Topics included $\infty$-categories, spectra, orientability, and applications in symplectic topology, concluding with discussions on stable categories of flow categories.
