# Global Kuranishi charts seminar

This is a physical seminar, taking place **Tuesday at 11:00 in Ross 70** (April–June 2026). The organizers are Keren and Arye.

The goal is to study [*Complex cobordism, Hamiltonian loops and global Kuranishi charts*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.14320) (by Abouzaid, McLean, Smith).  
The format is a reading seminar; each week a speaker prepares and presents a few pages from the paper.
