Floer homotopy seminar

This page contains materials from the Floer homotopy seminar (September–December 2024). The seminar is over, but the recordings and notes remain available for anyone interested. Organized with Sebastian Haney and Trygve Poppe Oldervoll, it was aimed at PhD students and postdocs in Homotopy Theory and Symplectic/Floer Theory. Topics included infinity categories, spectra, orientability, and applications in symplectic topology, concluding with discussions on stable categories of flow categories.
